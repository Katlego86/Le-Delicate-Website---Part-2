About Le Delicate

Beauty should feel personal.

A proposed studio built around confidence, creativity, care and practical learning.

Our story

Le Delicate was created as a proposed beauty services and training concept for this academic website project. The idea is to bring everyday beauty services and practical training into one welcoming brand.

Rather than making beauty feel complicated, the concept focuses on clear services, friendly communication and an experience that helps clients feel comfortable from the first enquiry.

Our mission

To create a welcoming beauty experience while making practical beauty learning accessible to people who want to develop new skills.

Our vision

To grow into a trusted beauty brand where quality service, creativity and learning can exist side by side.

Our values

  • Professionalism
  • Creativity
  • Respect
  • Customer care
  • Continuous learning

What matters to us

A thoughtful experience from start to finish

Listen first

Every appointment starts with understanding what the client wants rather than assuming a one-size-fits-all result.

Keep learning

Beauty changes quickly, so the concept encourages continued practice and learning.

Care about details

Small details such as communication, cleanliness and presentation can shape the whole client experience.
